## Introduction

We did this experiment twice: the first time during the 2026 spring program season at Kerrisdale, the second time for our summer program at Sunset. This activity can be frustrating sometimes when the carousels do not spin, but very cool to watch when it does. Usually, the vast majority of the class are able to get their carousels to work.

The setup and cleanup of this experiment is easy to medium. This is also relatively a low-cost experiment.

## Materials

- Round aluminum foil plates
- Paper with printed templates
- Markers
- Play-doh
- Scissors
- Wooden skewers
- Plastic straws

## Lesson

This lesson talked a bit about energy vs force, but mostly was a lesson on chemistry. We explained to the students how the candle carousel spins, as well as discussing why hot hair rises in general.
