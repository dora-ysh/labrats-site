## Introduction
This was the last activity in our LABRATS summer program. In this activity, we reviewed physics concepts and ran a paper roller coaster competition.

Both the setup and cleanup of this activity were easy compared to other experiments. 

## Materials

Here are all the materials we used:
- Scissors
- Marbles
- Tape
- Cardstock
- Template paper
We got our templates from [ScienceBuddies](https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/paper-roller-coaster) and printed them out on regular letter-sized paper.


## Lesson

Topics covered in our presentation included conservation of energy (which we reviewed from a previous week), and friction.

## Activity 

As mentioned earlier, we printed out templates for roller coaster parts from ScienceBuddies. To make the parts, students put a template on top of a piece of cardstock and cut it out.

While we gave students the templates, we didn’t give them any other instruction on how to make their roller coaster. Students got into groups of 2+ and came up with their own designs. We had a minimum height requirement, and otherwise awarded the prize to the group whose marble took the longest time to reach the ground.
