## Introduction

We did this experiment during our summer program at Kerrisdale. There is a setup required for this experiment, to build the launcher for the rockets. You can make the launcher using PVC pipe. [This](https://leftbraincraftbrain.com/how-to-make-diy-stomp-rockets/?cn-reloaded=1) was one of the sources we used to build our launcher.

We found that students generally liked this experiment, especially on a day with good weather, so that the rockets can be launched outside. Rockets can shoot several stories up, so it is not recommended to do it inside.

## Materials

- Launcher materials (including PVC pipe, 2L plastic bottle)
- Paper
- Scissors
- Markers

## Lesson

Topics covered in the presentation include the aerodynamics of rockets. Specifically, we discussed the physics of how our launcher system works, what forces act on a rocket, and what makes a rocket more aerodynamic.

## Activity 

We gave students some tips on how to make the fins and nose, but otherwise let students get into groups and experiment with their own designs. We gave students a chance to improve on their designs after the initial test. We recorded the launches and used the videos to judge which rockets went the highest.
